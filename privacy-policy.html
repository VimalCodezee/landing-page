Privacy Policy

Last updated on: 19 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Codezee Solutions Pvt. Ltd. collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use Codezee HRMS — our cloud-based human resources, payroll, and workforce management platform — through our website, web and mobile applications, and APIs (the "Services").

It applies to two audiences: Subscribers — organizations that use the Services to manage their workforce; and End Users — administrators, HR teams, line managers, employees, contractors, and candidates whose data is processed within a Subscriber's workspace.

By accessing or using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you are an employee of a Subscriber and have questions about your personal data, please contact your HR team first; we will assist your employer to resolve your request.

1. Controller & Processor Roles

Codezee plays different privacy roles depending on the data involved:

  • Controller — for marketing data (website visitors, demo requests), Subscriber account data (admin email, billing contact), and aggregated service operations data.
  • Processor — for Customer Data (employee records, payroll inputs, attendance logs, leave applications, performance reviews, uploaded documents). We process this data only on the Subscriber's documented instructions.

2. Data Collection & Processing

2.1 Information you provide directly.

  • Account & billing data: business name, GSTIN/Tax ID, registered address, admin name, work email, phone number, and payment method (card numbers are tokenized by our PCI-DSS-certified payment partners — we do not store full card numbers).
  • Helpdesk & support data: tickets, messages, attachments, and any screen recordings you voluntarily share during a support session.
  • Customer Data submitted by Subscribers: see Section 3 below.

2.2 Information collected automatically.

  • Device & technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, time zone, screen resolution, and referrer URL.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, features used, clickstream events, login times, session duration, and error logs.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: session cookies, authentication tokens, preference cookies, and analytics identifiers. See Section 8.
  • Location data (mobile app only): GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude), a reverse-geocoded street address, and device IP address — collected when a Subscriber enables location-based attendance features. May be collected in the foreground (user-initiated clock event) or in the background (server-triggered capture). See Section 3.3.

2.3 Information from third parties. Identity providers (Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra, Okta) when SSO is enabled; background verification vendors when initiated through our recruitment module; statutory portals and payroll gateways for confirmation of payouts; and marketing partners or events to which you have submitted your details.

3. Employee Data Handling

Codezee HRMS is purpose-built to handle sensitive workforce information. The Subscriber determines what employee data is processed; Codezee acts as the data processor.

3.1 Categories of employee data. Through the Services, the Subscriber may collect and store data such as:

  • Employee records: full name, employee ID, designation, department, date of joining, work and personal contact details, address, date of birth, gender, marital status, emergency contacts, and uploaded documents (offer letters, ID proofs, educational certificates, contracts).
  • Payroll & compensation: salary structures, CTC components, reimbursements, deductions, tax declarations, bank account details for salary disbursement, statutory IDs (PAN, UAN, ESIC, PF), and Form 16 data.
  • Attendance & leave: punch-in/punch-out timestamps, shift assignments, leave applications and balances. When the Subscriber enables location-based attendance, the app also collects GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude), a reverse-geocoded street address, and the device IP address at the time of each clock event. See Section 3.3 for full details.
  • Performance & engagement: goals, KPIs, OKRs, review ratings, manager and peer feedback, and engagement survey responses.
  • Recruitment & onboarding: candidate resumes, applications, interview feedback, assessment scores, and background verification results.

3.2 How we protect employee data.

  • Subscriber data is segregated by workspace, and internal access is restricted to authorized personnel through role-based access, MFA, and audit-logged just-in-time access grants.
  • Sensitive fields such as bank accounts and statutory IDs are additionally protected with field-level encryption.
  • Identifiers like Aadhaar are accepted as masked references where supported; non-production environments use anonymized synthetic data.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share employee data with third parties for their own marketing, and do not use it to train third-party AI models or advertising networks.

3.3 Location Data

What we collect. When the Subscriber activates location-based attendance features, the mobile application collects:

  • GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude) at medium-to-high accuracy via the device's GPS, Wi-Fi, and cell-tower sensors.
  • Reverse-geocoded street address derived from those coordinates using the device's native geocoding service.
  • Device IP address recorded at the time of each location event.
  • Timestamp of each capture, linked to the employee record.

How the app accesses location.

Mode When it happens Permission required
Foreground capture Employee actively taps Clock In or Clock Out inside the app While Using App
Background / server-triggered capture Administrator enables periodic location tracking; the server sends a silent push notification that wakes the app to record a position even when the app is closed or in the background Always Allow (Android: Allow all the time; iOS: Always)
Geo-fence validation On each clock attempt, the app computes the distance between the employee's position and the employer-configured office location(s) to verify proximity before accepting the punch While Using App

The app always requests explicit permission before accessing location. When the Subscriber enables background capture, the app specifically requests the "Always Allow" permission level and explains the reason at that time. Employees may deny or revoke this permission at any time in device settings; doing so will prevent background capture and may prevent clock-in from functioning if the Subscriber requires location verification.

Why we collect it.

  • To record a verified location stamp on each attendance clock event (clock-in, clock-out, break).
  • To enforce geo-fence policies configured by the employer — permitting or blocking a punch when the employee is outside an authorised work location.
  • To support periodic location tracking during work hours when explicitly enabled by the Subscriber, providing workforce visibility to authorised HR and line managers.

Who sees location data.

Location data is Customer Data (see Section 1). It is visible only within the employer's Subscriber workspace to users with the HR Manager, Line Manager, or Administrator role, as configured by the employer. Codezee does not access employee location data for its own commercial purposes, share it with advertisers, or use it to train any model.

Retention. Location records are retained for the duration configured by the Subscriber, subject to the minimums stated in Section 10 (Attendance & leave logs: minimum 3 years). Records are deleted in line with the post-termination schedule in Section 10.

Opting out. Location-based attendance is a feature the employer activates. Employees who have concerns about location collection should contact their HR department. Location permission may also be revoked on the device at any time via device Settings → App Permissions, though this may limit attendance functionality.

4. How We Use Information

We use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Provide the Services: authenticate users and run the HR, payroll, attendance, leave, performance, recruitment, and document workflows you configure.
  • Process payroll & statutory filings: calculate salaries, taxes, PF, ESI, professional tax, and TDS; generate pay slips, Form 16, and statutory reports.
  • Communicate with you: service announcements, security alerts, billing notices, product updates, and customer support.
  • Improve and secure the Services: diagnose bugs, monitor performance, detect abuse, prevent fraud, and conduct internal R&D using aggregated and de-identified data.
  • Marketing (Subscribers only): share product insights, webinars, and case studies — only where permitted by law and subject to your opt-out preferences.
  • Comply with law: meet our legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, and enforce our agreements.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we rely on contract, legitimate interests, legal obligation, consent, or vital interests, as appropriate. For Customer Data, the Subscriber determines the legal basis and we process on their instructions.

5. Sharing & Disclosure

We share personal data only when necessary and with appropriate safeguards:

  • Within your Subscriber workspace: employee data is visible to authorized colleagues based on the role configuration set by your administrator.
  • Service providers (sub-processors): cloud hosting, email delivery, SMS gateways, payment gateways, analytics, customer support, and security partners. A current sub-processor list is available upon request.
  • Integration partners: when you connect a third-party app, we exchange the minimum data required to operate the integration.
  • Professional advisors: auditors, legal counsel, and insurers under confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement & regulators: when reasonably required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Corporate transactions: in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case continued protection of personal data will be a condition of the transaction.

We do not sell personal data and do not share Customer Data with third-party advertisers.

6. Third-Party Integrations

The Codezee Platform supports optional integrations with services such as identity providers (Google, Microsoft Entra, Okta), accounting tools (Tally, Zoho Books, QuickBooks), communication platforms (Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business), biometric attendance devices, payout gateways, job boards, and background verification vendors. When you enable an integration, you authorize Codezee to exchange the minimum data required with that third party. Once data is in a third-party system, that vendor's privacy policy governs their processing. You can disconnect any integration from the admin console at any time.

7. Security Practices

Security is built into how we design, ship, and operate the Services. Our program is aligned with leading frameworks including SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001. Key safeguards include:

  • Encryption: TLS 1.2+ for data in transit and AES-256 for data at rest, with field-level encryption for sensitive identifiers such as bank account numbers and statutory IDs.
  • Access control: strict role-based access with MFA for privileged accounts, and granular permission templates for administrators.
  • Secure SDLC: code review, static and dynamic application security testing, dependency scanning, and periodic third-party penetration testing.
  • People & process: background checks, NDAs, and mandatory annual security and privacy training for all Codezee personnel.
  • Incident response: documented incident response plan with 24x7 on-call coverage for critical incidents.

If we become aware of a personal data breach that is likely to result in risk to individuals, we will notify affected Subscribers without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours.

8. Cookies & Session Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage, pixels, and SDKs) to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure traffic, and improve the Services. The main categories are:

  • Strictly necessary: authentication, session integrity, security, and fraud prevention (e.g., session ID, CSRF tokens, MFA verification).
  • Functional: remember your preferences, language, last-visited module, and theme.
  • Analytics: understand how users interact with the Services so we can improve them (anonymous page-view counters, feature adoption events, crash reports).
  • Marketing: measure ad campaigns on our public website only; never used inside the application.

You can manage non-essential cookies through the consent banner on our website or your browser settings. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent the Services from functioning.

9. International Data Transfers

Codezee primarily hosts Customer Data in data centers located in India. Where data is transferred internationally — for example, to provide global support or run regional disaster recovery — we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), Data Processing Agreements with sub-processors, and supplementary measures including encryption and access controls. For Subscribers with strict data residency requirements, we offer region-pinned hosting options under an enterprise order form.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

  • Active Subscriber accounts: for the duration of the subscription.
  • Employee records (active): for the duration of employment, as configured by the Subscriber.
  • Employee records (post-exit): up to 8 years after the exit date to support statutory audits (tax, PF, gratuity), configurable by the Subscriber.
  • Payroll & statutory filings: up to 8 years from the relevant financial year, as required under Indian law.
  • Attendance & leave logs: minimum 3 years; longer retention available based on Subscriber policy.
  • Recruitment (rejected candidates): up to 2 years, subject to candidate consent.
  • Marketing leads: up to 24 months from last engagement, or until you unsubscribe.
  • Server logs & security telemetry: up to 13 months.
  • Backups: encrypted snapshots retained for up to 180 days before being permanently overwritten.

After termination, Customer Data is available for export for 30 days, then deleted from active systems within 60 days and from backups within 180 days, subject to legal retention requirements.

11. Compliance Considerations

Codezee HRMS is built to help your organization meet its workforce-data compliance obligations. Our platform and practices are designed to support:

  • Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) — consent management, data principal rights, breach notification, and processor obligations.
  • EU GDPR & UK GDPR — lawful bases, cross-border transfers via SCCs, processor agreements, and data subject rights workflows.
  • SOC 2 and ISO/IEC 27001 — security, availability, confidentiality, and information security management.
  • PCI-DSS (via partners) — card data is tokenized and processed by certified payment partners.
  • Indian labour and tax law — statutory registers, Form 24Q, Form 16, ESI/PF challans, and Shops and Establishments compliance.

A Data Processing Addendum (DPA), sub-processor list, and security white paper are available to qualified Subscribers upon request.

12. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on the laws of your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority, including the Data Protection Board of India.

For Customer Data, please direct your request to your employer or Subscriber. Codezee will support Subscribers in responding to verified data subject requests. For data where Codezee is the controller (such as marketing data or Subscriber accounts), you can reach us at sales@codezeesolutions.com.

13. Children's Privacy

Codezee HRMS is designed for businesses and their adult workforce. The Services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, except where required to support legally compliant apprenticeship or intern programs initiated by a Subscriber.

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Services, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date, notify Subscriber administrators by email, and post an in-product banner. For significant changes, we will provide at least 30 days' notice before the changes take effect.
